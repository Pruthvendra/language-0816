## The Verification Era

By 2030, 65–80% of code will be AI-generated. But in 2026:

- Only **29% of developers trust AI output** (down from 40% in 2024)
- AI code carries **1.7× more defects**
- **45% of AI code** introduces OWASP vulnerabilities
- Developers spend **24% of their week** verifying AI output

The bottleneck has shifted from writing to **trusting**.

---

## Why We Exist

We do not exist because the world needs another programming language.

We exist because the world is about to drown in code it cannot verify.

Harven is the first language designed to make that bottleneck solvable.

---

## Core Beliefs

1. **Software should express human intent.**  
   Not accidental complexity. Not "vibe-coded" black boxes. Intent.

2. **Trust is the scarcest resource.**  
   By 2030, trust in software will be more valuable than speed.

3. **Security is not a feature.**  
   It is woven into the grammar. Developers must work intentionally to create insecure software.

4. **AI assists. Humans decide.**  
   AI accelerates engineering. Humans remain responsible for it.

5. **We build for decades.**  
   Every decision answers: "Will this still make sense when AGI handles complex tasks?"

6. **Readable survives. Clever dies.**  
   Readable software survives turnover, AI refactoring, and time.

---

## Success

Success is not downloads. It is when developers say:

&gt; "I trust Harven with software that matters — especially when AI wrote most of it."

---

## Our Promise

- We promise patience over hype.
- We promise documentation before expansion.
- We promise simplicity before addition.
- We promise that every line of code makes Harven easier to trust.

---

**The Founding Principle**

Build software that people trust.

Everything else is secondary.
