# Foundation Charter

## Purpose

This charter defines the mission and scope of the language-0816 foundation. It explains why the project exists, who the stakeholders are, and what success looks like.

## Scope

- Establish shared vocabulary and principles for the language-0816 community.
- Curate and maintain foundational documentation.
- Provide guidance for contributors and downstream projects.

## Governance

Describe roles, decision process, and how changes to core documents are proposed and accepted.
